# Domains

Short & clean:
  - filestem.com — "stem" = root/core + organic growth. Clean, memorable.
  - filenative.com — like "cloud-native" but for files. Modern, professional.
  - filekernel.com — life OS + files at the kernel level. Strong OS metaphor.

  Evocative:
  - fileorchestra.com — files orchestrating your life. Memorable image.
  - cortexfiles.com — brain/AI + files. Captures the intelligence angle well.
  - fileshaped.com — "file-shaped life". Concise, slightly poetic.
  - thoughtfiles.com — thoughts stored as files. Clean and clear.

  Identity-forward:
  - plaintextlife.com — strong minimalist/hacker brand. Life in plain text.
  - filedrivenlife.com — explicit, no ambiguity.

  Amplifier + identity ("superhuman" style):
  - kaihuman.com ⭐ — KAI-HU-MAN, exact rhythm of "superhuman". Kai (改) = kaizen, continuous improvement. "The ever-improving human." No brand confusion. Best find.
  - titanself.com — TI-TAN-SELF. Titan = legendary force. Powerful identity.
  - warphuman.com — WARP-HU-MAN. Beyond normal human limits. Edgy, tech feel.
  - shinehuman.com — SHINE-HU-MAN. The radiant/excellent version of you. More wellness-ish.
  - vasthuman.com — VAST-HU-MAN. Limitless human. Less obvious "better" meaning.
  - hyperdoer.com — HY-PER-DO-ER. The hyper action-taker. "Doer" is weaker identity word.

  "Sharpen yourself" angle (all verified available):
  - sapientself.com — Sapient = intelligent/wise (homo sapiens). Philosophical, strong.
  - geniself.com — Geni = genius. Short, punchy. "Your genius self."
  - lumenself.com — Lumen = light. "Your luminous self." Scientific yet evocative.
  - honeself.com — Hone = sharpen/refine. Direct, short, clear value prop.
  - sharpenself.com — Clear meaning, slightly plain.
  - upliftself.com — Positive, veers toward wellness.
  - acuityself.com — Acuity = mental sharpness. Slightly corporate.
  - chiselself.com — Chisel = sculpt/carve. Strong craft metaphor.
  - luminself.com — Luminous self. Beautiful coined word.
